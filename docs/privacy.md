# Raspberry 3D Viewer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Raspberry 3D Viewer is an offline 3D viewer for iPhone and iPad, published by FUJIAN AMERICAN ASSOCIATION OF NEW JERSEY A NJ NONPROFIT CORPORATION.

## Data collection

The iOS app does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, account registration, or a sign-in service.

The raspberry model, textures, and rendering resources are included with the app. Rendering, camera gestures, lighting adjustments, and reel playback happen locally on your device. The app does not require network access or access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or location.

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, or diagnostics according to Apple's own privacy policies and your device settings. That processing is separate from the app's features.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from children or other users.

## Questions and updates

For privacy questions or app support, visit the [Raspberry support page](https://github.com/guocity/raspberry/issues). Information you choose to post in a public GitHub issue is visible to others and is handled under GitHub's privacy policy.

Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
